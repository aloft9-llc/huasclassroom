Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Find areas in $\text{cm}^2$ by counting $1\ \text{cm}^2$ squares.
  2. 02Use area = length × width for rectangles.
  3. 03Solve problems (pool, largest square from a rectangle).

Core Concepts

Move from counting squares to the rectangle/square area formula.

Count cm²

Each small square is $1\ \text{cm}^2$; count to find the area.

Length × Width

$$\text{area of rectangle} = \text{length} \times \text{width}$$
$$\text{area of square} = \text{side} \times \text{side}$$

Rectangle Problems

Length $40\ \text{cm}$, $10\ \text{cm}$ longer than width; pool length twice width; cut largest square from $12\ \text{cm}$ craft paper.
