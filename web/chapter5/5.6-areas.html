Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Measure area by counting unit grid squares.
  2. 02Find areas of rooms on a floor plan.
  3. 03Compare open spaces / figures on grids.
