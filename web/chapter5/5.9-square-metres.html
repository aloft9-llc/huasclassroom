Core Concepts

Introduce $\text{m}^2$ for larger spaces and apply to fields.

Square Metre

$$1\ \text{m}^2 = \text{area of a }1\ \text{m}\times 1\ \text{m}\text{ square}$$

Side $100\ \text{cm}$ → area $1\ \text{m}^2$.

Sense of Size

About one pupil can stand in $1\ \text{m}^2$. Perimeter stays fixed if the same wire forms different closed shapes — area can change.

Field Applications

Wheat field width $25\ \text{m}$, length = times width; yield per $\text{m}^2$ → total harvest.
