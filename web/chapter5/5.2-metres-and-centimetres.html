Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Know $1\ \text{m} = 100\ \text{cm}$.
  2. 02Convert and compare mixed metre–centimetre lengths.
  3. 03Solve ribbon / length word problems.

Core Concepts

Work fluently between metres and centimetres.

Metre & Centimetre

$$1\ \text{metre} = 100\ \text{centimetres}$$

Convert & Compare

$810\ \text{cm}$ vs $81\ \text{m}$; $6\ \text{m}\ 57\ \text{cm}$ vs $675\ \text{cm}$; add $28\ \text{m}+500\ \text{cm}$.

Applications

Ribbon $800\ \text{cm}$ plus another length — answer in cm and m.
