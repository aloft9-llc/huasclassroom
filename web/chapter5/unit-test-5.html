Lesson Alignment

Unit Test 5 reviews Chapter 5 — measurement, symmetry, triangles, and area.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Convert and compare metric lengths; compute fluently.
  2. 02Apply symmetry and triangle classification language.
  3. 03Solve area problems including walls with windows.
  4. 04Check units ($\text{cm}^2$ vs $\text{m}^2$).
