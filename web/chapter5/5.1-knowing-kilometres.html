Core Concepts

Introduce the kilometre and convert with metres.

Kilometre

$$1\ \text{kilometre} = 1000\ \text{metres}$$

Use for longer distances (home → school → library).

Convert km ↔ m

Examples: $8\ \text{km}$, $4000\ \text{m}$, $5\ \text{km}+2\ \text{km}$, mixed $1\ \text{km}\ 780\ \text{m}$.

Applications

Order distances; total walk Home–School–Library ($1800\ \text{m}+1200\ \text{m}$).
