Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Classify triangles as acute-, right-, or obtuse-angled.
  2. 02Identify isosceles and equilateral triangles and their symmetry.
  3. 03Count equilateral triangles hidden in a diagram.

Core Concepts

Sort triangles by angles and by sides; connect to symmetry.

By Angles

Acute-angled

All angles acute.

Right-angled

One right angle.

Obtuse-angled

One obtuse angle.

By Sides

Isosceles: at least two equal sides. Equilateral: all sides equal (also equiangular).

Draw & Count

Draw isosceles/equilateral with lines of symmetry; count equilateral triangles in a figure.
