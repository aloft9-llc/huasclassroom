Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Know $1\ \text{dm} = 10\ \text{cm}$ and relate to m / km / mm.
  2. 02Choose equal lengths among mixed units.
  3. 03Estimate heights and solve rope × times problems.

Core Concepts

Place the decimetre on the metric ladder and compare carefully.

Decimetre

$$1\ \text{dm} = 10\ \text{cm},\quad 10\ \text{dm} = 1\ \text{m}$$

Metric Ladder

Watch traps: $30\ \text{cm}\neq3\ \text{mm}$; $3\ \text{m}\ 6\ \text{cm}=306\ \text{cm}$ (not $306\ \text{dm}$).

Estimate & MCQ

Order lengths; estimate a person’s height; rope $3\ \text{m}\ 5\ \text{dm}$ used 8 times.
