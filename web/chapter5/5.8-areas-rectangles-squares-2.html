Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Calculate areas or missing side lengths.
  2. 02Reason about how changing length changes area.
  3. 03Find areas of frames and shaded regions with midpoints.

Core Concepts

Deepen rectangle/square area — changes, frames, shaded parts.

Compute Areas

Given sides in cm, find areas; find a missing side when area is known.

Changing Length

Length $18\ \text{cm}$; length $+5\ \text{cm}$ increases area by $40\ \text{cm}^2$ → find original width (draw a diagram).

Composite / Shaded

Photo frame border; midpoints of square sides — area of shaded part.
