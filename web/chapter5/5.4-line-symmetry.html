Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Identify shapes with line symmetry and draw lines of symmetry.
  2. 02Recall how many lines of symmetry common shapes have.
  3. 03Complete the other half of a symmetric figure; explore capital letters.

Core Concepts

Recognise reflection symmetry and count lines of symmetry.

Line of Symmetry

A fold line where both halves match exactly.

Squares, Rectangles, Triangles

  • Square: $4$ lines of symmetry.
  • Rectangle (not square): $2$.
  • Equilateral triangle: $3$.

Letters & Folding

Which capital letters are symmetric? Fold a square twice and punch a hole — predict the pattern when unfolded.
