Core Concepts

Tree-planting / interval problems — objects vs gaps.

Intervals vs Objects

Sawing wood into $7$ parts needs $6$ cuts. $n$ trees in a line → usually $n-1$ gaps.

Both Sides of a Path

$40$ trees on both sides including ends, $8\ \text{m}$ apart → pavement length.

Around a Lake

Perimeter $2100\ \text{m}$, a tree every $10\ \text{m}$ → how many trees around the lake?
