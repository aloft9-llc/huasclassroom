Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Read a floor plan and identify rooms.
  2. 02Calculate areas of rooms and composite figures.
  3. 03Draw a rectangle with the same area as a given square; solve fence perimeter problems.

Core Concepts

Apply area to a real floor plan and related perimeter/area problems.

Read the Floor Plan

Balcony, office, kitchen, dining room, etc. — use given dimensions.

Room Areas

Find each room; combine for totals where asked.

Same-Area Rectangles

Draw a rectangle equal in area to a square; field length $20\ \text{m}$, width half length; $20\ \text{m}$ fencing for a vegetable plot.

Key answers
  1. Floor plan areas: Xiao Pang $12$; parents $16$; lounge $36$; dining $9$; kitchen $6$; balconies $6$; bathroom $6$; hallway $7$; total $98$ m²
  2. Same area rectangle: e.g. $8\times2$ (area $16$ cm²)
  3. L-figure area: follow labelled dimensions (workbook $65$ cm²)
  4. Fill brackets: (1) cm²; cm;\; (2) $110$ cm² remaining;\; (3) width $1$ cm
  5. Applications: (1) room $12$ m²;\; (2) field $200$ m²;\; (3) max fence area $25$ m²
  6. Enhancement: (6) large rectangle $336$ cm²;\; (7) original land $17680$ m²
