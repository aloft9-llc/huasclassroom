Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Identify the repeating unit in a shape or number pattern.
  2. 02Find the $n$th term (e.g. 102nd).
  3. 03Sum the first several terms when useful.
