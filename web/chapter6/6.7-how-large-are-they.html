Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Find areas by counting grid squares in $\text{cm}^2$.
  2. 02Use length/width relationships between rectangles.
  3. 03Reason about larger/smaller rectangles from clues.

  1. Grid areas: (1) $15$ cm²;\; (2) $21$ cm²;\; (3) $6$ cm²;\; (4) $11$ cm²

  4. Two offices: larger $42$ m²; smaller $28$ m²
  5. Four rectangles: $?=500$ cm²
  6. Squares A & B: A $144$ cm²; B $81$ cm²
