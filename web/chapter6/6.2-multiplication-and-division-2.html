Division Relationship

$$\text{dividend} = \text{divisor} \times \text{quotient} + \text{remainder}$$

Example: divisor $65$, quotient $8$, remainder $9$.

Mixed Fluency

Column practice continuing from 6.1.

Track & Volume Stories

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

Key answers
  1. Column method: $430\times6=2580$;\; $620\div4=155$;\; $560\div8=70$;\; $537\div9=59\,\mathrm{r}\,6$
  2. Step by step: $462\times7+367=3601$;\; $780\times5-2564=1336$;\; $700-700\div5=560$;\; $658+328+572+142=1700$;\; $432+629+268-229=1100$
  3. Number sentences: (1) $1000\div8=125$;\; (2) $10\times10\div10=10$;\; (3) $465\times5=2325$
  4. Dividend: $65\times8+9=529$
  5. Applications
    • (1) Drunk $1520$ ml; left $2280$ ml
    • (2) $30$ years younger next year
    • (3) ① Yes ($78\times6=468<500$); ② change $15$ yuan; ③ gave $300$ yuan
    • (4) Xiao Ya $1600$ m; Dingding $6$ laps, $800$ m more; total $4000$ m
  6. Enhancement: footballs $10$; basketballs $30$
  7. Cryptarithm: $A=1$, $B=2$, $C=5$, $D=6$ ($125\times5=625$)
