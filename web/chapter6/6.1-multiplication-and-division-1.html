Students will be able to:

  1. 01Calculate multi-digit × and ÷ with the column method.
  2. 02Check starred items carefully.
  3. 03Solve sharing and comparison applications (cards, running).

Core Concepts

Consolidate multiplication and division algorithms from Chapters 2 and 4.

Column × and ÷

Vertical calculation with self-check on marked items.

Check Answers

Use inverse operations: quotient × divisor + remainder; estimate products.

Applications

$673$ cards ÷ $6$ with $13$ left; compare runners’ speeds from a table; spend exactly $54$ yuan on pens.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

Key answers
  1. Column method: $89\times6=534$;\; $801\div9=89$;\; $8\times356=2848$;\; $293\times5=1465$;\; $769\div7=109\,\mathrm{r}\,6$;\; $464\div8=58$
  2. Step by step: $6\times187-216=906$;\; $585\div5+78=195$;\; $1010-288\div8=974$;\; $367+205\times8=2007$;\; $240+240\div6=280$;\; $408\times7+973=3829$
  3. Fill in the brackets
    • (1) between $200$ and $240$; product $236$
    • (2) $370\times3\times2=2220$
    • (3) $990\div3\div3$
    • (4) $14$ (other factor $20$; $20-6=14$)
  4. Cards: $673-13=660$;\; $660\div6=110$ cards each
  5. Running: Pang $135$ m/min; Dingding $96$ m/min; Ming $136$ m/min. Fastest: Xiao Ming; slowest: Xiao Dingding
  6. Hairy crabs: $96$ more crabs; pay $198\times30=5940$ yuan
  7. Enhancement — pens for $54$ yuan (10 ways): $(18,9,6)$-counts = $(3,0,0)$, $(0,6,0)$, $(0,0,9)$, $(2,2,0)$, $(2,0,3)$, $(1,4,0)$, $(1,0,6)$, $(0,4,3)$, $(0,2,6)$, $(1,2,3)$
  8. Fill boxes: (1) $785\times9=7065$;\; (2) $822\div3=274$
