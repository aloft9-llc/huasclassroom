Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Recall month lengths, quarters, and leap-year rules.
  2. 02Read monthly calendars to answer date/weekday questions.
  3. 03Solve multi-step application problems.
  4. 04Connect calendar facts with ×÷/+− fluency.

Core Concepts

Practice and exercise — mixed review of Chapter 3 time and calendar skills.

Chapter Skills Review

  • Big/small months; February in common vs leap year.
  • Quarters and months in each quarter.
  • Leap-year rule (÷4; century ÷400).

Reading Calendars

From a monthly grid: name the month, find Sundays, deduce the previous month’s last Sunday, etc.

Applications

  • Average savings per month across quarters.
  • Theatre seats (rows × seats + ground floor) — mixed ops with calendar unit.
