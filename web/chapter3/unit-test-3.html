Core Concepts

Unit Test 3 assesses Chapter 3 — year, month, day, leap year, and calendar use.

Fluency & Calendar

  • Read two consecutive monthly calendars; answer date questions.
  • Leap year identification among given years.

MCQ Focus

  • Maximum days in two consecutive months (careful with Feb).
  • Which year is a leap year (watch 2300 vs 2060).
