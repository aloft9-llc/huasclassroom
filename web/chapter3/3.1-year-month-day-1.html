Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01State today’s date including weekday. (Question 1)
  2. 02Record the number of days in each month. (Question 2)
  3. 03Connect holidays to month, date, and weekday.
  4. 04Count days / Wednesdays across a date range. (Enhancement)

Core Concepts

Read and record dates: year, month, day, and day of the week.

Year, Month, Day

  • A year has 12 months.
  • Write today’s date: day of the month, month name, year, and weekday.

Days in Each Month

Students record days for each month this year (table: Jan–Dec).

TypeDaysMonths (typical)
31-day months$31$Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Aug, Oct, Dec
30-day months$30$Apr, Jun, Sep, Nov
February$28$ or $29$Common year / leap year
Preview: February $29$ appears in a leap year (taught fully in 3.3).

Days of the Week

There are 7 days in a week. Connect holidays to weekday (e.g. Halloween 31 October).

Applications

  • When does the second Sunday in May fall? (Mother’s Day style questions)
  • Length of summer holidays / school term in days; how many Wednesdays?

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Sample: 20th · October · 2025 · Monday; 12 months; 31 days this month.

2. Jan 31, Feb 28/29, Mar 31, Apr 30, May 31, Jun 30, Jul 31, Aug 31, Sep 30, Oct 31, Nov 30, Dec 31.

3. Jul & Aug · 31 · big months Jan/Mar/May/Oct/Dec; Children’s Day 1 Jun · 30 · small Apr/Sep/Nov; fewest Feb 28–29; year 365 days.

4–5. 7 days/week; holidays & birthdays student-specific (sample: Halloween 31 Oct; Thanksgiving 27 Nov; Christmas 25 Dec; birthday 5 Mar).

6. 2025–2034 Feb: 28×3, 29 (2028), 28×3, 29 (2032), 28×2. Feb usually 28, else 29; if 28 → 365 = 52 weeks + 1 day. Birthday-on-leap: 29 Feb (e.g. 2016).

7. 1 May 2020 Friday → second Sunday 10 May.

8. Summer holidays — student-specific.

9. Sample: 1 Aug → 9 Jan = 162 days; ≥23 Wednesdays.

10. June with 4 Wed & 5 Tue → 1 June Monday.
