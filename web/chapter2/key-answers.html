2.1 · Multiplying whole tens and whole hundreds (1)

Open lesson notes →

Key answers
  1. Calculate with reasoning
    • $4×7=28$, $4×70=280$, $4×700=2800$
    • $5×3=15$, $50×3=150$, $500×3=1500$
    • $6×9=54$, $60×9=540$, $6×90=540$
  2. Fill in the brackets
    • (1) $240$; $4×60=240$.   $1000$; $5×200=1000$
    • (2) $3$ tens; $18$ tens; $180$
    • (3) $3$ zeros ($800×5=4000$)
  3. Compare: (1) $=$   (2) $>$   (3) $<$   (4) $<$   (5) $<$   (6) $=$
  4. Match: $5×70→350$; $500×7→3500$; $7000×5→35000$; $5×7→35$
  5. Fill suitable numbers: (1) $400$   (2) $2$   (3) $5$   (4) $4$
  6. True / false: (1) $×$ (false)   (2) $✓$ (true; $270<280$)
  7. Number sentences: (1) $600×3=1800$   (2) $400×8+8=3208$
  8. Applications: (1) $4$ kg   (2) page $81$
  9. Extension: $2400$ yuan
