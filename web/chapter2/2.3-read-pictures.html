Core Concepts

Read a picture or story, write a multiplication number sentence, then calculate.

From Pictures to Sentences

  1. What is one group? How many in each group?
  2. How many groups?
  3. Write $\text{groups} \times \text{size}$ (or size × groups) and solve.
Example: 5 people × 30 yuan → $5 \times 30 = 150$ yuan.

Match Questions to Equations

Same picture can ask different questions (cost of 2 boxes vs number of cartons in 2 boxes). Match each question to the correct sentence.

Applications

  • Distance: 2 laps × 50 m.
  • Remainder seating: total − (6 coaches × 35).
  • Compare: $6 \times 8 - 4 \times 8$ postcards.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

Key answers
  1. Write number sentences
    • (1) $2+1+3=6$ people; $30×6=180$ yuan
    • (2) $12×3=36$ yuan
  2. Match
    • (1) $2×70$   (2) $2×45$   (3) $2×12$   (4) $4×10$
  3. Applications
    • (1) $50×4=200$ m (two laps)
    • (2) $35×6=210$; $234-210=24$ pupils
    • (3) $48-32=16$ postcards
  4. Find the buddies: $39×8=312$; $26×3=78$; $92×4=368$; leftover $276$ (e.g. $138×2$)
  5. Open condition (sample): “There are $0$ chocolates”; $0×8=0$ boxes
  6. Greatest product (pairs sum to $16$): $8$ and $8$; product $64$
