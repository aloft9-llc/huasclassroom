  3. 03Apply properties to simplify expressions.

Core Concepts

Practice and exercise — mixed review of Chapter 2 multiplication skills.

Chapter Skills Review

  • Whole tens/hundreds × one-digit ($40 \times 5$, $280 \times 2$).
  • Two-digit × one-digit ($12 \times 7$, $56 \times 6$).
  • Three-digit × one-digit and mixed +/−/× expressions.

Horizontal & Column

Use the horizontal method and column method as directed; rewrite expressions with properties when helpful ($56 \times 7 + 7 \times 44$).

Applications

Family ticket prices, chrysanthemum show, and other multi-step money stories from the lesson.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

Key answers
  1. Mental: $200$; $84$; $120$; $133$; $280$; $90$; $560$; $4$; $8$
  2. Horizontal: $356×2=712$; $637×3=1911$
  3. Column: $68×4=272$; $7×56=392$; $5×61=305$; $605×8=4840$; $6×473=2838$; $4×350=1400$
  4. Step by step: $626$; $403$; $675$; $700$; $734$; $1899$
  5. Fill brackets: (1) $4$   (2) between $240$ and $300$, nearer $300$   (3) $9900$   (4) $125$
  6. Applications: (1) $32400$ yuan   (2) $77$ photos   (3) $100$ yuan   (4) $720$ books; not enough ($840$ needed)
  7. Extension: (1) $7$ zeros   (2) greatest $642×8$; least $468×2$
