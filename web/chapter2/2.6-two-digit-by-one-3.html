Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Calculate mixed mental items involving × and +/−. (Question 1)
  2. 02Use column method and correct errors. (Question 2–4)
  3. 03Match sentences to products. (Question 5)
  4. 04Create and solve open multiplication sentences. (Enhancement)

Core Concepts

Consolidate two-digit × one-digit — mental links, matching, and open number sentences.

Mental & Mixed Fluency

Combine multiplication with +/− using order of operations, e.g. $2 \times 8 + 4$, $9 \times 3 + 3$.

Match Products

Connect number sentences to answers ($33 \times 6$, $25 \times 7$, …) — reinforces fact fluency and algorithms.

Open Sentences & Digit Play

  • Fill digits so a vertical product is true.
  • Use 4, 5, 6 to write a two-digit × one-digit sentence and compute.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

Key answers
  1. Mental: $20$; $30$; $53$; $53$; $61$; $41$; $52$; $3$
  2. Column: $77×4=308$; $9×36=324$; $25×8=200$; $4×75=300$
  3. Estimate then calculate
    • $49×7$: between $280$ and $350$; $=343$
    • $75×6$: between $420$ and $480$; $=450$
    • $88×8$: between $640$ and $720$; $=704$
  4. Corrections: $99×9=891$ (correct); $26×8=208$; $68×5=340$
  5. Match: $33×6=198$; $25×7=175$; $56×4=224$; $9×42=378$; $5×63=315$
  6. Applications: (1) $57×6=342$ yuan   (2) $28×3=84$ kWh
  7. $190×8=1520$
  8. (sample) $65×4=260$
