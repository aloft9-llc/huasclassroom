Core Concepts

Unit Test 2 assesses Chapter 2 — multiplying by a one-digit number.

Fluency Checklist

  • Whole tens/hundreds × one-digit ($9 \times 30$, $800 \times 5$).
  • Two- and three-digit × one-digit; mixed $2 \times 30 + 3$.
  • Compare with $>$ $<$ $=$ ($27 \times 6$ vs $26 \times 7$, $250 \times 4$ vs $8 \times 125$).

Compare & Sentences

  • Unknown number from quotient (÷7 = 650 → ×7).
  • Product of two given factors; sum of equal addends then subtract.
