Core Concepts

A compact review map of what Unit Test 1 assesses from Lessons 1.1–1.5.

Mental & Column Fluency

  • Mental: ×÷ before +−; left-to-right for same-level ops.
  • Column method: line up place values; watch borrowing across zeros (e.g. $1002 - 888$).
Common error: $1002 - 888$ is $114$, not $224$ — check each borrow.

Properties & Order

Regroup subtraction

$$472 - 148 - 152 \\ = 472 - (148 + 152)$$

Friendly pairs

$$213 - 43 + 87 \\ = (213 + 87) - 43$$

Order of operations

$$6 \times 7 - 4 \times 5 \\ = 42 - 20$$

Geometry Review

  • Greatest angle acute ⇒ acute-angled triangle.
  • Place value: in $808$, the two $8$s differ by a factor of $100$ (hundreds vs ones).
  • Angle size does not decide triangle “size” — don’t confuse angle type with area.
  • Multi-square blocks: can they tile a $4 \times 3$? Check area $12$ and fit.
