Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Trace the edge of shapes to feel perimeter. (Question 1)
  2. 02Calculate perimeter on a grid. (Question 2)
  3. 03Solve laps around a regular polygon. (Question 3)

Core Concepts

Perimeter is the distance around a shape.

What is Perimeter?

The total length of the boundary (edge) of a shape.

Grid Perimeters

Count outer unit segments carefully — do not count inner lines.

Laps & Polygons

Regular pentagon side 30 m × 5 × 6 laps = distance run.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

Key answers

  1. Trace edges (drawing).
  2. Multiple choice: (1) B (rope); (2) A (straight ruler).
  3. Grid perimeters (1 m squares): Shape 1: 14 m; Shape 2: 16 m; Shape 3: 20 m; Shape 4: 24 m.
  4. Perimeters: triangle 17 cm; quadrilateral 17 dm; parallelogram 26 dm; regular hexagon 54 m.
  5. Pond: $30\times5\times6=900$ m.
  6. Enhancement: 5 combinations; shortest perimeter 8 cm.
