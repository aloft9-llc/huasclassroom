Core Concepts

Find perimeters of combined shapes; cutting can change P and A differently.

Composite Perimeter

Add outer edges only — shared inner edges cancel.

Cut & Compare

Cutting the same square from two figures may keep perimeters equal while areas differ (or vice versa).

Same String

Square side 18 cm → P = 72 cm = rectangle with length 19 → width = $(72\div2)-19$.
