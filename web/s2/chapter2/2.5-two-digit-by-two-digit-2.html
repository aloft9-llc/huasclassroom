Core Concepts

Column multiply; inverse stories (take out half in 10 steps).

Column Method

Partial products: ones × both digits of second factor, then tens × both; add.

Number Sentences

“Product of two eighty-nines” → $89 \times 89$; greatest two-digit sum × 9.

Seating Problems

Row/seat position from left and right → seats per row → total capacity.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Column fill-in

$48\times65=3120$ (ones digit $5$, tens digit $6$)

2. Column method

  • $13\times22=286$;\; $44\times73=3212$;\; $77\times68=5236$

3. Corrections

  • $44\times55=2420$;\; $37\times12=444$;\; $26\times98=2548$

4. Number sentences

  1. $89\times89=7921$
  2. $(99+99)\div9=22$

5. Application

  1. $212$ Grade 5 pupils
  2. $1028$ yuan; yes ($588<600$)
  3. $704$ seats
  4. Whole road $1280\,\text{m}$

6. Enhancement

  • Squares: $121,144,169,196,225,256,289,324,361$
