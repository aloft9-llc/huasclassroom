Core Concepts

Multi-step reverse problems: half repaired day 1, etc.

Work Backwards

Day 2 is twice day 3; day 1 is half the road — draw a bar model.

Road Repair

Known remainder + daily amounts → total length.

Equal Area

Same area, different length → find missing width by division.

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

  • $82\times(5)<483$;\; $78\times(8)<645$;\; $64\times(8)<521$

  • $560\div59=9\cdots29$;\; $260\div28=9\cdots8$;\; $445\div45=9\cdots40$

3. Estimate then calculate

  • $333\div35=9\cdots18$;\; $606\div66=9\cdots12$;\; $515\div59=8\cdots43$
