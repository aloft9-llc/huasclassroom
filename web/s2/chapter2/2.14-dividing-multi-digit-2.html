Core Concepts

Mixed expressions; $84\times14\div49$; capsule every 4 hours.

Mixed Operations

$125 \times 32 \div 72$ — calculate left to right unless brackets group steps.

Order of Operations

“Product of 84 and 14, divided by 49” → $(84 \times 14) \div 49$.

Time Schedule

Capsule every 4 hours — count backwards from dose 11 to find start time.
