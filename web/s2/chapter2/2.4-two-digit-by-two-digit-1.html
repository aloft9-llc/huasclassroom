  2. 02Use splitting ($48\times25 = 48\times20 + 48\times5$). (Question 2)

Core Concepts

Introduce two-digit × two-digit: mental $10\times$, split, column.

Methods

Same product: column, split, or regroup ($5\times24 + 20\times24$).

Split

$48 \times 25 = 48 \times (20 + 5)$ using distributive property.

Mental ×10

$16 \times 10$, $24 \times 10$, $12 \times 100$ — shift place value.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Mental / boxes

  • $16\times10=160$,\; $16\times5=80$,\; sum $240$;\; $16\times10+16\times15=16\times25$
  • $24\times10=240$,\; $24\times5=120$,\; sum $360$;\; $24\times10+24\times5=24\times15$
  • $33\times10=330$,\; $33\times5=165$,\; sum $495$;\; $33\times10+33\times5=33\times15$

2. Estimate then calculate

  1. $73\times42=3066$
  2. $29\times67=1943$

3. Different methods for $48\times25$

All give $1200$

4. Calculate

  • $19\times27=513$;\; $43\times77=3311$;\; $31\times19=589$

5. Number sentences

  1. $550-19\times19=189$
  2. $99\times100=9900$

6. Application

  1. $160$ balls
  2. Grandpa $72$ years old
  3. Yes — cost $11200$ yuan ($<12000$)

7. Enhancement

$142857\times3=428571$: $A=4$,\; $B=2$,\; $C=8$,\; $D=5$,\; $E=7$
