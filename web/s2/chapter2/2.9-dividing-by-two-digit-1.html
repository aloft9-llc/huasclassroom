Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Mental

  • $6\div2=3$,\; $60\div2=30$,\; $60\div20=3$; similarly $8/80/40$,\; $9/90/30$,\; $21/210/70$

2. Think carefully

  1. $99\div23=4\cdots7$
  2. $517\div63=8\cdots13$

3. Column method

  • $174\div21=8\cdots6$;\; $195\div53=3\cdots36$;\; $132\div22=6$
  • $347\div43=8\cdots3$;\; $608\div86=7\cdots6$

4. Application

  1. 1st goat, 2nd cat, 3rd elephant (same distance $\Rightarrow$ compare speeds)
  2. Daily averages $9$,\; $12$,\; $14$

5. Enhancement — hire vehicles

  1. $4$ coaches, $1920$ yuan
  2. $11$ minibuses, $2200$ yuan
  3. Prefer coaches (cheaper); mix $3$ coaches $+2$ minibuses $=1840$ yuan also possible
