Core Concepts

Three-digit × two-digit column; place-value alignment.

Column Method

Same as two-digit × two-digit, but first factor has hundreds digit.

Place Value

Shift tens partial product one place left before adding.

Money Problems

“124 yuan less than 17 times the microwave” — two-step total cost.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Mental (around $125$)

  • $125\times3=375$ … $125\times11=1375$ (and related sums as on the page)

2. Estimate then calculate

$229\times67=15343$

3. Column fill-in

$418\times65=27170$;\; $123\times5=615$,\; $123\times40=492$

4. Column method

  • $357\times89=31773$;\; $256\times19=4864$;\; $555\times99=54945$;\; $188\times62=11656$

5. Number sentences

  1. $222\times55=12210$
  2. $999\times99=98901$

6. Application

Fridge $6659$ yuan; together $7058$ yuan

7. Enhancement

  1. $n5\times n5$: $25,225,625,1225,2025,3025,4225,5625,7225,9025$

  3. Convoy length $514\,\text{m}$
