Core Concepts

Identify wholes and parts; introduce fraction language.

Whole and Part

A whole can be one object or a set. A part is a piece of that whole.

Fraction Language

“One fifth” means the whole is divided into 5 equal parts; take 1.

Draw Equal Parts

Parts must be equal in size (area or number).

Key Terms & Definitions (Reference)

Quick glossary for this lesson.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Wholes and parts

Each pair: left = whole; right = part.

2. Draw one part

Example: $8$ of the $16$ stars (half the whole), or any proper subset drawn from the whole.

3. Let’s draw

Whole: e.g. a square in $4$ equal parts; Part: shade $1$ part ($\frac{1}{4}$).

4. Line symmetry

Mirror the given half across each dashed line (house/arrow; smiley; circle; hexagon).
