Core Concepts

Non-unit fractions: several equal parts of a whole.

Non-Unit Fractions

$\frac{3}{4}$ means 3 parts of size $\frac{1}{4}$.

From Pictures

Count shaded equal parts over total equal parts.

Match Shading

Check both numerator (how many) and denominator (equal parts).

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Fractions from pictures

  1. Apples $\frac{1}{9}$; strawberries $\frac{5}{9}$; apples and bananas $\frac{4}{9}$.
  2. Black $\frac{3}{12}$; white $\frac{9}{12}$.

2. Shaded parts

$\frac{4}{9}$;\; $\frac{1}{3}$;\; $\frac{2}{8}$ (or $\frac{1}{4}$).

3. Fill in

  1. Each piece $\frac{1}{7}\,\mathrm{m}$; $4$ pieces $\frac{4}{7}\,\mathrm{m}$.
  2. $5$ lots of $\frac{1}{8}$ are five eighths.
  3. $\frac{6}{10}$.
  4. $3$ lots of $\frac{1}{9}$ in $\frac{3}{9}$.
  5. $2$;\; $3$;\; $6$ triangles.

4. Judge

(1) ✕;\; (2) ✓;\; (3) ✓.

5–7. Enhancement

  • Each piece $\frac{1}{6}$ of the length; each sawing $\frac{1}{5}$ of the total time.
  • Grandma $\frac{1}{12}$; sister $\frac{3}{12}$; brother $\frac{3}{12}$; Mum $\frac{5}{12}$.
  • $\frac{2}{18}$ of $36=4$;\; $6$;\; $8$;\; $12$;\; $18$;\; $24$.
  • Still to deliver: $100\,\mathrm{kg}$.
