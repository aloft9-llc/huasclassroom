Core Concepts

Unit fractions: one equal part of a whole.

What is a Unit Fraction?

$\frac{1}{n}$ means 1 of $n$ equal parts.

Read & Write

Say “one fifth”; write $\frac{1}{5}$.

Colour the Part

Shade exactly one equal part of the whole.

Key Terms & Definitions (Reference)

Quick glossary for this lesson.

TermDefinition
Unit fractionA fraction with numerator 1.
DenominatorHow many equal parts the whole is divided into.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Read and write

  1. $\frac{1}{4}$: one fourth;\; $\frac{1}{50}$: one fiftieth.
  2. One fifth $\frac{1}{5}$; one third $\frac{1}{3}$.
  3. One tenth $\frac{1}{10}$; whole divided into $10$ equal parts; each is $\frac{1}{10}$.

2. Equally divided?

✓, ✕, ✕, ✕, ✓

3. Shaded parts

$\frac{1}{8}$;\; $\frac{1}{4}$;\; $\frac{1}{3}$.

4. Colour / circle

Shade/circle $1$ of $8$; $1$ of $3$ stars; $1$ of $4$ triangles.

5. Unit fractions

$\frac{1}{2}$;\; $\frac{1}{6}$;\; $\frac{1}{3}$;\; $\frac{1}{4}$.

6–8. Enhancement

  • Class size example: $15$ pupils; you $\frac{1}{15}$; girls $\frac{6}{15}$ (use your class).
  • Two days: $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}=\frac{3}{4}$ of the book.
  • Folded $3$ times → $8$ equal parts; each is one eighth $= \frac{1}{8}$.
