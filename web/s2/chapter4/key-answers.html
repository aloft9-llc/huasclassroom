Chapter 4 · Introduction to fractions (I)

Answer key for this chapter’s lessons (from marked workbook pages).

4.1 · Whole and part

Open lesson notes →

1. Wholes and parts

Each pair: left = whole; right = part.

2. Draw one part

Example: $8$ of the $16$ stars (half the whole), or any proper subset drawn from the whole.

3. Let’s draw

Whole: e.g. a square in $4$ equal parts; Part: shade $1$ part ($\frac{1}{4}$).

4. Line symmetry

Mirror the given half across each dashed line (house/arrow; smiley; circle; hexagon).
