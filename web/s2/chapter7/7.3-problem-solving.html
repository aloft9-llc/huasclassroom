Core Concepts

Road repair, speed, production, and camp grouping stories.

Times as Many

Repaired 156 m; remaining is 5 times repaired → total = $156 + 5\times156$.

Rates

Speed and production-per-day stories — multiply or divide carefully.

Grouping

420 pupils, 30 per class → classes; then “not in competition”.
