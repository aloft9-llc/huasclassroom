Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

Key answers

  1. Column: $25\times47=1175$; $508\times290=147320$; $340\times890=302600$; $958\div29=33$ r 1; $6760\div52=130$; $6860\div67=102$ r 26.
  2. Cards 8,7,5,4: e.g. greatest $84\times75=6300$; least $47\times58=2726$ (pairings using each digit once).
  3. Step by step: 1740; 584; 26; 37.
  4. Sentences: $625-(875\div25)=590$; $265\times14+100=3810$.
  5. Fill in: 9900; 4-digit quotient with 2 middle zeros (2002); $\triangle=24$, $\square=324$; one-digit quotient: 1–4; two-digit: 5–9.
  6. MCQ: B; A.
  7. Enhancement: greatest $52\times43=2236$, least $24\times35=840$; $12346\times67889$ is greater.
