Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Find area when perimeter and one side are known. (Question 1)
  2. 02Compare rectangle vs square from the same wire. (Question 2)
  3. 03Compute area increase when length and width grow. (Question 3)

Core Concepts

Given perimeter find area; same wire → different shapes; enlarge sides.

From P to A

$P=66$, one side 22 → other side $=66\div2-22$, then area.

Same Wire

Same perimeter string: rectangle $9\times3$ vs square — compare areas.

Enlarge Sides

Increase length and width by 5 dm — draw before/after to find extra area.
