Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01List ways to place 3 shirts into 2 wardrobes. (Question 1)
  2. 02List ways to place 4 peaches onto 3 plates. (Question 2)
  3. 03Use a table to organise cases. (Question 1–2)

Core Concepts

Place items into boxes/plates; list all distributions.

Distribution Table

Columns for each container; rows for each way.

All Cases

Work systematically: (3,0), (2,1), (1,2), (0,3) for two wardrobes.

Allow Empty?

Check whether empty containers are allowed in the problem.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

Key answers

  1. 4 ways: (0,3), (1,2), (2,1), (3,0).
  2. $C(4,2)=6$ choices.
  3. $C(3,2)=3$ combinations.
  4. 15 ways (identical peaches, distinct plates).
  5. At least 2 born in the same month.
  6. Take out 6 balls.
  7. Maximum 12 pupils.
