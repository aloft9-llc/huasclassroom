How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Count boy–girl host pairs. (Question 1)
  2. 02Count games if every two play once. (Question 2)
  3. 03Order river-crossing steps. (Question 3)

Core Concepts

Count pairings, games, and river-crossing step orders.

Pair Selections

Choose 1 boy from B and 1 girl from G → $B \times G$ ways.

Tournament Games

Every two of 5 play once → $5\times4\div2 = 10$ games.

Arrange the Order

River crossing: dog, rabbit, grass — sequence steps so nothing is eaten.

  7. Order: A → D → B → C.
  8. Tickets 72; fares 36.
  9. 16 ways (compositions of 5).
  10. 72 ways on $4\times4$ grid.
