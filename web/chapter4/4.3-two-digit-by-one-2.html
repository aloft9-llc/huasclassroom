Core Concepts

Connect the vertical algorithm to equal sharing by place value.

Column Method Meaning

Example MCQ: in $46 \div 2$, the marked part may mean “40 pencils have been given” — not “4 remaining.”

Place-Value Steps

  1. Divide tens.
  2. Bring down ones; divide ones.
  3. Record quotient and any remainder.

Applications

Cages for rabbits; mushrooms per week from a 3-day rate.
