Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Mental calculation

  • $8\times30=240$,\; $300\times6=1800$,\; $540\div5=108$,\; $400\div8=50$,\; $5400\div6=900$
  • $40\times40=1600$,\; $50\div10=5$,\; $200\div4=50$,\; $170+130=300$,\; $360\div4=90$
  • $24\times5-100=20$,\; $8\times2\times4=64$,\; $29-3\times8=5$,\; $62-56\div7=54$,\; $6\times6\div9=4$
  • $7\times8+45=101$,\; $80\div4-17=3$,\; $12-8\div2=8$,\; $40+6\times9=94$,\; $10+48\div8=16$

2. Column method

  • $826\div9=91\ldots7$;\; $4\times276=1104$;\; $780\div6=130$;\; $919\div8=114\ldots7$

3. Step by step

  • $2001-978\div3=1675$;\; $318+182\times5=1228$;\; $456\div8\times4=228$
  • $54\times15+15\times46=1500$;\; $371+369+(231+29)=1000$;\; $175+25\times4=275$

4. Write the number sentences and calculate

  1. $130$
  2. $251$
  3. $23$
  4. $1346$
  5. $104$

5. Fill in the brackets

  1. fifty-four divided by three
  2. tens place;\; $2$-digit
  3. Least $(6)$;\; greatest for $2$-digit $(5)$
  4. Total $(30)$
  5. Least divisor $(8)$;\; dividend $(135)$
  6. $(3)$ zeros
  7. $(7)$ possible values

6–12. Application

  1. $79$ bottles each;\; $2$ left
  2. $82$ books;\; need $6$ more pieces
  3. $2650$ kg
  4. $56$ red flowers per day
  5. $7$ more boxes each day
  6. $120$ kg;\; $96$ kg more
  7. $430$ yuan

13–15. Enhancement

  1. Story-book × $26$;\; Notebook $1$ × $31$
  2. $\star=9$ ($19+91+9=119$)
  3. Three lines into $6$ parts of $2$ players each (e.g. $1$ vertical $+\;2$ horizontal)
