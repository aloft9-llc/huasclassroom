Core Concepts

Introduce the relationship among unit price, quantity, and total.

Price Triangle

$$\text{total price} = \text{unit price} \times \text{quantity}$$

So unit price $= \text{total} \div \text{quantity}$, quantity $= \text{total} \div \text{unit price}$.

Find Any One

Boxes of apples: cost of 8 boxes; apples in 8 boxes; rubbers bought with given money.

Compare Unit Prices

Different children buy different packs — compute price per rubber and compare.
