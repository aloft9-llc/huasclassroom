Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Solve multi-step problems mixing × and ÷ with prices.
  2. 02Combine quantities (rice + flour sacks by mass).
  3. 03Plan purchases from a price list within a budget.

Core Concepts

Extend unit-price reasoning into multi-step and measurement contexts.

Multi-Step Price Problems

Step-by-step expressions mixing × and ÷ (e.g. $96 \div 3 \times \ldots$).

Mass × Sacks

$9$ sacks × $20$ kg rice + flour sacks × $35$ kg → total mass.

Budget Stories

Dad gives different amounts to children to buy stationery from a price list.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Work these out step by step

  • $8\times35+8\times25=480$;\; $96\div3\times5=160$;\; $336\div4\div6=14$
  • $978+345\div5=1047$;\; $999-99\div9=988$;\; $25\times4\div4\div5=5$

2. Fill in the brackets

  1. $(6)$ yuan — unit price
  2. $(3)$ volleyballs — quantity
  3. $(648)$ yuan — total price
