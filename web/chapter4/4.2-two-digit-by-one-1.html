Core Concepts

Introduce two-digit ÷ one-digit with division vocabulary and remainders.

Parts of Division

$$\text{dividend} = \text{divisor} \times \text{quotient} + \text{remainder}$$

Remainder must be less than the divisor.

Mental Division

Use known facts and “how many groups of …” language.

Remainder Language

Fill: when dividend is 23 and divisor is __, quotient and remainder are given.

Applications

Baskets of apples; children cutting flowers — average / equal share.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Mental division

  • $9\div2=4\ldots1$,\; $25\div4=6\ldots1$,\; $27\div5=5\ldots2$,\; $38\div6=6\ldots2$
  • $19\div3=6\ldots1$,\; $36\div5=7\ldots1$,\; $39\div4=9\ldots3$,\; $47\div5=9\ldots2$

2. Greatest number in the brackets

  • $6\times(5)<32$,\; $(6)\times9<60$,\; $(6)\times8<53$
  • $8\times(6)<50$,\; $7\times(8)<62$,\; $(8)\times9<78$

3. Sharing sweets

$73\div5=14\ldots3$

4. Let’s try

  • $64\div4=16$,\; $92\div4=23$,\; $84\div7=12$,\; $81\div7=11\ldots4$,\; $86\div6=14\ldots2$

5. Fill in the brackets

  1. $48\div4$ is read as forty-eight divided by four
  2. $27$ is $(3)$ times $9$;\; $72$ is $(8)$ times $9$
  3. There are $(8)$ eights in $64$;\; $64$ is $(8)$ times $8$
  4. Quotient $(5)$, remainder $(3)$
  5. Divisor $(6)$
  6. Dividend $(48)$
  7. $(8)$ less than $22$

6. Write the number sentences and calculate

  1. $75\div5=15$
  2. $75\times5=375$

7. Application problems

  1. $25\times4=100>96$ — yes, can finish on time
  2. $88\div4=22$ stamps
  3. $64\div4=16$ apples per basket
  4. Average of three: $17$;\; with Fangfang: $18$

8–9. Enhancement

  • $4-4+4-4=0$;\; $(4-4)+(4\div4)=1$;\; $(4\div4)+(4\div4)=2$;\; $(4+4+4)\div4=3$;\; $((4\times4)+4)\div4=5$;\; $(4\div4)+(4+4)=9$
  • $(1+2)\div3=1$;\; $1\times2+3-4=1$;\; $1+2-3-4+5=1$;\; $1+2+3-4+5-6=1$
