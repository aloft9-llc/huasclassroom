Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Check whether a division is correct using multiplication.
  2. 02Solve shopping / sharing problems with remainders.
  3. 03Decide how to treat leftovers in context (can’t buy a fraction of a case).

Core Concepts

Apply division in real contexts and emphasise checking.

Checking Division

Verify $251 \div \ldots$ style items: quotient × divisor + remainder = dividend.

Money Sharing

100 yuan for pencil cases at a given price — how many can he buy? (remainder = leftover money)

Sense-Making

Quotient is whole packs/cases; remainder is unused money or leftover items.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Mental division

  • $17\div3=5\ldots2$,\; $33\div7=4\ldots5$,\; $43\div8=5\ldots3$,\; $29\div9=3\ldots2$
  • $52\div6=8\ldots4$,\; $75\div10=7\ldots5$,\; $25\div4=6\ldots1$,\; $42\div5=8\ldots2$

2. Column method

  • $502\div5=100\ldots2$,\; $486\div8=60\ldots6$,\; $520\div5=104$,\; $725\div6=120\ldots5$

3. Write the number sentences and calculate

  1. Quotient $123$, remainder $3$
  2. Sum $270$
  3. $102$ times

4. Fill in the brackets

  1. $(83)\times(3)+(2)=(251)$
  2. $(2)$ zeros ($8000\div5=1600$)
  3. Quotient $(1)$;\; divisor $(1)$;\; dividend $(0)$
  4. At most $(16)$ notebooks
  5. $(52)$ weeks $+\;(1)$ day

5. Application problems

  1. At least $24$ boxes
  2. Each class $6$ boats;\; sharing: $11$ boats
  3. $12$ pencil cases
  4. $18$ exercise books;\; need $5$ more pieces

6–7. Enhancement

  1. $250$ kg dried fish
  2. Yes — $6$ cuts × $5=30$ minutes
