Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Mental calculation

  • $30\times2=60$,\; $46\div2=23$,\; $21\times3=63$,\; $80\div4=20$
  • $120\times3=360$,\; $300\times5=1500$,\; $840\div4=210$,\; $50\times4=200$
  • $9000\div3=3000$,\; $60\div9=6\ldots6$,\; $240\div6=40$,\; $500\times9=4500$
  • $2000\times4=8000$,\; $1000\times7=7000$,\; $2000\div5=400$,\; $10000\div5=2000$

2. Column method (check)

  • $58\div4=14\ldots2$,\; $61\div2=30\ldots1$,\; $92\div5=18\ldots2$
  • $37\div3=12\ldots1$,\; $97\div6=16\ldots1$,\; $74\div7=10\ldots4$

3. Application problems

  1. $16$ black sheep
  2. $9$ days
  3. $12$ minutes
  4. $21$ groups
  5. Yes;\; $6$ empty cans left
  6. $21$ pages each day (remaining $6$ days)
  7. $60$ yuan for $12$ balls;\; $18$ balls for $90$ yuan

4–5. Enhancement

  • $31\div7=4\ldots3$;\; $31\div4=7\ldots3$
  • $39\div7=5\ldots4$;\; e.g. $71\div8=8\ldots7$
