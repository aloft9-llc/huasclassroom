Core Concepts

Harder three-digit ÷ problems: comparisons and recovering unknowns.

“Times as Many” with ÷

152 literature books is exactly 8 times comic books → comics $= 152 \div 8$.

Find the Unknown

÷5 gives quotient 123 remainder 2 → number $= 5 \times 123 + 2$.

Full Relationship

Quotient = remainder = 3; divisor = 3 × remainder → find dividend.
