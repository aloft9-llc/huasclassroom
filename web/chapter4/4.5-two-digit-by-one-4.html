Lesson Alignment

How this Shanghai Maths lesson connects to learning goals, Common Core, and Eureka Math.

Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

  1. 01Divide two-digit ÷ one-digit fluently (mental + written).
  2. 02Translate language into division sentences (“What is 82 ÷ 2?”).
  3. 03Reason through the enhancement mouse / every-other puzzle.

Key Answers

Quick reference for this lesson’s exercise answers.

1. Mental calculation

  • $30\div3=10$,\; $40\div2=20$,\; $80\div4=20$,\; $60\div2=30$,\; $500\div5=100$
  • $39\div3=13$,\; $48\div2=24$,\; $84\div4=21$,\; $68\div2=34$,\; $6000\div3=2000$

2. Calculate and compare

  • $96\div3=32$,\; $92\div3=30\ldots2$,\; $96\div6=16$,\; $92\div6=15\ldots2$

3. Column method

  • $83\div4=20\ldots3$,\; $81\div2=40\ldots1$,\; $65\div6=10\ldots5$,\; $90\div4=22\ldots2$
  • $96\div9=10\ldots6$,\; $53\div5=10\ldots3$,\; $75\div7=10\ldots5$,\; $87\div8=10\ldots7$

4. Division charts

  • $480\div4=120$,\; $480\div6=80$,\; $480\div8=60$
  • $3000\div3=1000$,\; $3000\div5=600$,\; $3000\div6=500$

5. Related operations

  • $2\times200=400$,\; $400\div2=200$;\; $300\times3=900$,\; $900\div3=300$;\; $4\times500=2000$,\; $2000\div4=500$

6. Write the number sentences and calculate

  1. $82\div2=41$
  2. $900\div3=300$
  3. $640\div2=320$
  4. $320\div4=80$

7. Application problems

  1. $180$ cubic metres per hour
  2. $75$ flies each in $3$ hours;\; $50$ flies per hour for $2$ dragonflies;\; $25$ flies per dragonfly per hour

8. Enhancement

The white mouse stood in the 8th place.
