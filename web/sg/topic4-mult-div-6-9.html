Learning objectives

  • 1Match related × and ÷ sentences fact families for 6–9.
  • 2Complete patterns that encode ×6, ×7, ×8, ×9 relationships.
  • 3Compute products and quotients with remainders where needed.
  • 4Use model drawings for multi-step grouping / sharing problems.
  • 5Apply these facts in word problems and Take the Challenge! items.

Core concepts

Fact family matching

$$8 \times 7 = 56 \;\Leftrightarrow\; 56 \div 7 = 8 \;\Leftrightarrow\; 56 \div 8 = 7$$

Remainder language

$$677 \div 9 = 75\ \mathrm{R}\ 2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 9 \times 75 + 2 = 677$$
Model habit: Draw equal groups or comparison bars before choosing × or ÷.

Key terms

Fact family, product, quotient, remainder, factor, related number sentences, model drawing.
