Topic 2 · Addition and Subtraction

Multi-digit ± to 10,000 — same fluency habits as Shanghai Chapter 1 column work.

Addition & subtraction Shanghai S1 · Ch1 Eureka G3 M2 Pages 12–27

Lesson alignment

How this Singapore practice topic reinforces Shanghai Maths ideas and maps to Common Core / Eureka — the same note structure as One Lesson One Exercise.

Companion book: Shanghai Maths — One Lesson One Exercise, Grade 3 (ECNU Press English).

Learning objectives

  • 1Add and subtract multi-digit numbers to 10,000 with regrouping — including cross-number puzzles.
  • 2Find sums and differences from language (“the sum of…”, “the difference between…”).
  • 3Translate place-value units into addends/subtrahends (e.g. 8 thousands + 41 tens).
  • 4Reason about missing digits and form greatest/smallest numbers from given digits.
  • 5Solve multi-step word problems and Take the Challenge! items that link ± with place value.

Core concepts

Parts of addition / subtraction

Addition

$$\text{Addend} + \text{Addend} = \text{Sum}$$

Subtraction

$$\text{Minuend} - \text{Subtrahend} = \text{Difference}$$

Unit language before calculating

$$\text{8 thousands} + \text{41 tens} = 8000 + 410 = 8410$$
$$\text{11 hundreds} - \text{42 tens} = 1100 - 420 = 680$$
Strategy: Estimate first, then column check. For “difference between greatest and smallest,” identify extremes, then subtract once.

Key terms

Sum, difference, addend, minuend, subtrahend, regrouping, cross-number puzzle.
