Topic 5 · Money

Money Place value + apps Shanghai S1 applications Pages 58–70

Lesson alignment

How this Singapore practice topic reinforces Shanghai Maths ideas and maps to Common Core / Eureka — the same note structure as One Lesson One Exercise.

Companion book: Shanghai Maths — One Lesson One Exercise, Grade 3 (ECNU Press English).

Learning objectives

  • 1Read prices in dollars and cents and separate dollar / cent parts.
  • 2Find the value of mixed notes and coins in a purse.
  • 3Write amounts in figures and in words.
  • 4Compose amounts with given coin types; ± / × money in context.
  • 5Solve shopping word problems (change, totals, comparisons).
