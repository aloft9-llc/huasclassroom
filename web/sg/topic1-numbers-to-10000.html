Topic 1 · Numbers to 10,000

Place value to 10,000 — reinforced with Shanghai Maths Chapter 1 ideas.

Place value Shanghai S1 · Ch1 Eureka G2 M3 / G4 M1 Pages 1–11

Lesson alignment

How this Singapore practice topic reinforces Shanghai Maths ideas and maps to Common Core / Eureka — the same note structure as One Lesson One Exercise.

Companion book: Shanghai Maths — One Lesson One Exercise, Grade 3 (ECNU Press English).

Learning objectives

  • 1Read, write, and decompose 4-digit numerals into thousands, hundreds, tens, and ones.
  • 2State place value “the digit 5 stands for 500” — and compare / order numbers to 10,000.
  • 3Complete number patterns and reason about “more than / less than” with place-value units.
  • 4Solve word problems that pack or count with hundreds and thousands (bills, bags, cartons).
  • 5Take the Challenge! Digit puzzles and spacing problems that deepen number sense.
