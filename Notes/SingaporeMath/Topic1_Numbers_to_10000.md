## Lesson Alignment

How this Singapore practice topic reinforces Shanghai Maths ideas and maps to Common Core / Eureka.

### Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

1. **Read, write, and decompose** 4-digit numerals into thousands, hundreds, tens, and ones.
2. **State place value** (“the digit 5 stands for 500”) and compare / order numbers to 10,000.
3. **Complete number patterns** and reason about “more than / less than” with place-value units.
4. **Solve word problems** that pack or count with hundreds and thousands (bills, bags, cartons).
5. **Take the Challenge!** — digit puzzles and spacing / line problems that deepen number sense.

## Core Concepts

### Place-value chart

```
  Thousands │ Hundreds │ Tens │ Ones
      8     │    9     │  2   │  3     → 8923
```

\[
8923 = 8000 + 900 + 20 + 3
\]

### “Stands for” vs digit

In **4578**, the digit **5** stands for **500** (not “5”).

### Unit language

| Language | Think | Number |
|----------|-------|--------|
| 5 tens more than 750 | \(750 + 50\) | \(800\) |
| 2 hundreds less than 7033 | \(7033 - 200\) | \(6833\) |
| 50 tens of spectators | \(50 \times 10 = 500\) | use in word problems |

### Challenge habits

- Digit sums + “twice / three times” constraints → systematic trial.
- Lights spaced \(20\,\mathrm{m}\) apart: \(n\) lights → \(n-1\) gaps.
