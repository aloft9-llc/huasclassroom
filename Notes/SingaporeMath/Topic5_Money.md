## Lesson Alignment

### Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

1. **Read prices** in dollars and cents and separate dollar / cent parts.
2. **Find the value** of mixed notes and coins in a purse.
3. **Write amounts** in figures and in words.
4. **Compose amounts** with given coin types; add / subtract / multiply money in context.
5. **Solve shopping word problems** (change, totals, comparisons).

## Core Concepts

### Dollars and cents

\[
\$19.05 = 19 \text{ dollars} + 05 \text{ cents}
\]

100 cents = 1 dollar.

### Writing in words / figures

| Figures | Words |
|---------|-------|
| \(\$20.09\) | twenty dollars and nine cents |
| \(\$0.91\) | zero dollars and ninety-one cents |

### Alignment habit

Line up the decimal points (or think in cents) before adding or subtracting.
