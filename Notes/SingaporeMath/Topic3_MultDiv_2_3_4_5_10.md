## Lesson Alignment

### Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

1. **Extend skip-counting** and number patterns for factors 2, 3, 4, 5, and 10.
2. **Write number sentences** for equal-groups stories (bags, rows, passengers).
3. **Use bar / model drawings** for “times as many” comparisons.
4. **Multiply and divide** within these tables, including multi-step calendar-style problems.
5. **Take the Challenge!** items that deepen inverse ×÷ thinking.

## Core Concepts

### Equal groups → number sentence

\[
4 \text{ bags} \times 5 \text{ apples} = 20 \quad;\quad 45 \div 5 = 9 \text{ each}
\]

### “Times as many” bar model

```
Brother:  |=====|  5
Ned:      |=====|=====|=====|  3 × 5 = 15
```

### Fact family (example)

\[
4 \times 9 = 36,\quad 9 \times 4 = 36,\quad 36 \div 4 = 9,\quad 36 \div 9 = 4
\]
