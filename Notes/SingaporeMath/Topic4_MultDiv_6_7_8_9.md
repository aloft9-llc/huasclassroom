Students will be able to:

1. **Match related × and ÷ sentences** (fact families for 6–9).

3. **Compute products and quotients** with remainders where needed.
4. **Use model drawings** for multi-step grouping / sharing problems.
5. **Apply** these facts in word problems and Take the Challenge! items.

## Core Concepts

### Fact family matching

\[
8 \times 7 = 56 \;\Leftrightarrow\; 56 \div 7 = 8 \;\Leftrightarrow\; 56 \div 8 = 7
\]

### Remainder language

\[
677 \div 9 = 75\ \mathrm{R}\ 2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 9 \times 75 + 2 = 677
\]

### Model habit

Draw equal groups or comparison bars **before** choosing × or ÷.
