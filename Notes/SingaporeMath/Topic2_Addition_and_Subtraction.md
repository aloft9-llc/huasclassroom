## Lesson Alignment

### Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

1. **Add and subtract** multi-digit numbers (to 10,000) with regrouping — including cross-number puzzles.
2. **Find sums and differences** from language (“the sum of…”, “the difference between…”).
3. **Translate place-value units** into addends/subtrahends (e.g. 8 thousands + 41 tens).
4. **Reason about missing digits** and form greatest/smallest numbers from given digits.
5. **Solve multi-step word problems** and Take the Challenge! items that link ± with place value.

## Core Concepts

### Parts of addition / subtraction

```
   Addend + Addend = Sum
Minuend − Subtrahend = Difference
```

### Unit language before calculating

\[
\text{8 thousands} + \text{41 tens} = 8000 + 410 = 8410
\]

\[
\text{11 hundreds} - \text{42 tens} = 1100 - 420 = 680
\]

### Strategy habits

- Estimate first (front-end / friendly numbers), then column check.
- “Difference between greatest and smallest” → identify extremes, then subtract once.
