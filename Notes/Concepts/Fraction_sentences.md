# Concept guide  
## Understanding fraction sentences

**Focus:** How to read three common fraction question patterns  
**Links:** Shanghai Maths Grade 3 · Second Semester Chapter 4 (fractions)

---

## Three patterns

| Pattern | Example | Find |
|---------|---------|------|
| **A.** Fraction of a number | $\frac{5}{7}$ of $35$ | The **part** |
| **B.** What part of… | What part of $15$ is $11$? | The **fraction** |
| **C.** …of what number | $18$ is $\frac{2}{3}$ of what number? | The **whole** |

---

## A · $\frac{5}{7}$ of $35$

Whole known. Divide into $7$ equal parts, take $5$.

$$
\frac{5}{7}\text{ of }35 = 35 \div 7 \times 5 = 25
$$

---

## B · What part of $15$ is $11$?

Whole $= 15$ (after “of”). Part $= 11$.

$$
\frac{\text{part}}{\text{whole}} = \frac{11}{15}
$$

---

## C · $18$ is $\frac{2}{3}$ of what number?

Part known. Find one third, then the whole.

$$
18 \div 2 \times 3 = 27
$$

---

## Reading habit

1. Underline “of.”  
2. Name known / unknown (part, fraction, or whole).  
3. Say the sentence in plain English.  
4. Draw equal boxes for the denominator.  
5. Check the answer makes sense.
