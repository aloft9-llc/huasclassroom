### Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

1. **Calculate** expressions with more than two × or ÷ factors. (Question 1)

4. **Interpret** models as different groupings of the same product. (Question 4)
5. **Solve multi-step word problems** that chain multiplication or division. (Question 5, 6)

## Core Concepts

### Order of operations

× ÷ left to right, then + −.

### Combining sentences

\[
2 \times 4 = 8,\ 8 \times 3 = 24 \Rightarrow (2 \times 4) \times 3 = 24
\]

### Associative property

\[
(a \times b) \times c = a \times (b \times c)
\]

### Models

Same product, different groupings: \((2 \times 5) \times 3 = 2 \times (3 \times 5) = 30\).

## Key Terms

Mixed number sentence, parentheses, associative property, factor, product, equal groups.
