## Core Concepts

### Cuboid & Cube

| Solid | Vertices | Faces | Edges | Figure |
|-------|----------|-------|-------|--------|
| Cuboid | 8 | 6 | 12 | Longer box (rectangular faces) |
| Cube | 8 | 6 (all squares) | 12 | Equal edges; all faces squares |

**Vertices, faces & edges**

- **Vertex** — a corner where edges meet
- **Face** — a flat side of the solid
- **Edge** — a line where two faces meet

### Square

4 equal sides; 4 right angles → special rectangle.

### Units

**Mass:** $1\ \text{kg} = 1000\ \text{g} = 1\,000\,000\ \text{mg}$

**Length:** $1\ \text{km} = 1000\ \text{m} = 100\,000\ \text{cm} = 1\,000\,000\ \text{mm}$

**Time:** $1\ \text{hour} = 60\ \text{minutes} = 3600\ \text{seconds}$

### Reading a clock

- **Hour hand** — shorter; shows the hour (moves slowly).
- **Minute hand** — longer; each number = 5 minutes.
- **O'clock** — minute hand on 12; **quarter past** — on 3; **half past** — on 6; **quarter to** — on 9.
- At **half past three** (3:30), the hour hand sits between 3 and 4, so the smaller angle between the hands is **75°**, not a right angle. (Question 2)

### Triangle types

Acute / right / obtuse — classified by the **greatest** angle.
