## Lesson Alignment

### Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

1. **Name** two-, three-, and four-square blocks. (Question 1)
2. **Grow** a block by adding one square (2→3, 3→4). (Question 2)
3. **Compose** a \(4 \times 3\) rectangle and a \(4 \times 4\) square. (Question 2)
4. **Find combinations** that make a \(3 \times 5\) rectangle. (Question 2)
5. **Tile** a larger square using given blocks, each once. (Enhancement)
