## Key Terms

- **Whole tens:** A multiple of 10 (e.g. 30, 70).
- **Whole hundreds:** A multiple of 100 (e.g. 500, 800).
- **Factor / Product:** Numbers multiplied / the result.
- **Trailing zeros:** Zeros at the end of a product.
