Hello, I’m Hua

I hold a B.A. in Communicative Disorders and Sciences and a Master of Library and Information Science (MLIS) from San José State University. My background has given me a strong foundation in language, learning, and organizing complex information into clear, accessible explanations. I currently work as a teacher assistant, supporting elementary students with their homework after school.

Working with children every day has taught me that mathematics is about much more than memorizing procedures or getting the right answer. Students learn best when they understand why a method works, recognize patterns, and develop confidence through thoughtful practice.

This website grew out of my own experience helping students with their daily homework. I often found myself searching for clear explanations of the concepts in their workbooks—how each lesson builds mathematical understanding, how it connects to previous learning, and how it aligns with the Common Core State Standards and the Eureka Math curriculum.

Hua's Classroom is designed as a practical companion for parents, teachers, tutors, and anyone supporting a child's mathematical learning. Each chapter explains key mathematical ideas in clear language, provides a quick reference for reviewing lessons, and helps families better understand the knowledge and skills students are expected to develop.

My hope is that these notes make mathematics more understandable, less intimidating, and more enjoyable for every child—and for the adults who guide their learning.
